What to review

Move the head sliders. Earth-bearing ticks and velocity markers remain tied to the outside world. The aircraft panel stays at the nose. Looking away reveals an integrated aircraft-attitude instrument. Move back toward the nose to restore the forward scales.

The forward marker is an open ring with wings. The opposite velocity direction adds a cross. Neither is a steering command. IAS is never replaced by groundspeed. Pressure, geometric MSL, barometric, and relative altitudes retain their labels.
