What to review

Move the head sliders. Earth-bearing ticks and velocity markers remain tied to the outside world. The aircraft panel stays at the nose. Looking away reveals an integrated aircraft-attitude instrument. Automation status and alerts retain display-fixed positions. Move back toward the nose to restore the forward scales.

The forward marker is an open ring with wings. The opposite velocity direction adds a cross. Neither is a steering command. IAS is never replaced by groundspeed. Pressure, geometric MSL, barometric, and relative altitudes retain their labels.

Display profiles never command the aircraft. Confirmed HUD and cockpit profiles simulate current visibility evidence for another flight display. Primary flight information returns when evidence expires, data fails, or urgent alerts appear. Mission mode reserves space; no radar or weapon source is connected.

Flight samples are frozen for review. Mode-event attention runs once from the supplied event age. Use Replay mode event to restart that review timeline. Changing the viewpoint or profile does not restart it.
